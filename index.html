Marshgleam

You are a little green marsh frog. Point with your mouse or finger and the frog hops after it. Click, tap or press Space to flick your tongue at flies, beetles and darting dragonflies. Chain catches for combos, snap up glowing fireflies for swift legs, and find the rare lotus for a protective shield. Hold Shift to run, press X or right-click to leap, and tap again in mid-air to flap and fly. Watch for the hawk's shadow ring, grow from Tadpole to Marsh King, and beware the water snake, the night and the rain.
